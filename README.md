# Long-Range-nu-Interaction
This project investigates neutrino oscillations modified by a new long-range potential mediated by an ultra-light boson $Z'$, associated with gauge symmetries such as $L_e - L_\mu$. The interaction is sourced by electrons distributed inside the Earth, and its effects are evaluated by computing the modified matter potential and solving the Schrödinger equation for three-flavor evolution.

The Earth's internal structure is modeled using the PREM electron density profile, and custom numerical routines are implemented for both the 3D integration of the new potential and the flavor evolution. Oscillation probabilities are computed and compared with and without the new interaction to show the impact of such long-range forces on neutrino propagation.
